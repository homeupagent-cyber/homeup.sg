How the numbers work
  • Must sell at. Price plus Buyer's Stamp Duty plus ABSD, grown at the target return for the holding period, grossed up for selling costs, divided by size.
  • Cushion at exit. The proven exit psf (capped by the next buyer's budget if entered), grown at the area growth rate to the exit year, minus the must-sell-at psf, times size.
  • Walk-away price. The highest psf where the cushion is still zero or better, also capped by the lease-adjusted resale premium limit when comparables exist.
  • Beats closing now below. The psf where the alternative's cushion, plus the client's own-stay preference, equals the close-now cushion.
  • The waiting bet. For each scenario: if he gets a good unit, and the alternative is below walk-away and better than coming back, he takes it. Otherwise he returns to the close-now project at a higher price and possibly a worse unit. Each path is weighted by your chances.
  • Lease reset. SLA leasehold table, freehold at 100%. Years not in the published extract are interpolated, accurate to about half a point.
  • Not modelled. Mortgage interest, progressive payment timing, rental income, SSD, and differences in stack, view or facing within each project.

Financing and rent

Holding period, growth, costs and ABSD are shared with the Compare tab.

How this tab works
  • Same budget. The new launch unit's price. Each resale option buys as many sqft as that budget allows at its average psf, so stamp duty and loan size are identical.
  • Lease decay to exit. Each option's exit psf is today's psf grown at the area rate, scaled by the SLA table from its lease left today to its lease left at exit. Freehold does not decay.
  • Newness premium. The new launch's premium over the lease-adjusted resale average. It holds until TOP, then fades in a straight line to zero over the years you set.
  • Interest. Resale pays interest on the full loan from day one. The new launch pays on your drawn share until TOP, then the full loan.
  • Rent. Resale earns or saves rent from day one. The new launch only after TOP.
  • Net gain. Exit value after selling costs, minus price and duties, minus interest, plus rent. Per year is net gain divided by total cost and years held.
  • Not modelled. Maintenance fees, property tax, renovation, vacancy, and differences in layout, facing or facilities.
